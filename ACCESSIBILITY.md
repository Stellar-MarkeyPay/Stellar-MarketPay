# Accessibility Statement

Stellar MarketPay is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.

## Target Standard

The Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) defines requirements for designers and developers to improve accessibility for people with disabilities. It defines three levels of conformance: Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. Stellar MarketPay targets conformance with **WCAG 2.1 level AA**.

## Known Limitations

While we strive for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, we are aware of some limitations:

- Certain third-party integrations (like wallet connection modals) may not fully comply with all WCAG 2.1 AA standards, as they are outside our direct control.
- Complex data tables showing historical transaction data may not be fully responsive or optimized for mobile screen readers on smaller devices.
- Some legacy UI components may lack complete ARIA labels. We are actively working to audit and update these components.

## Reporting an Accessibility Barrier

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Stellar MarketPay. If you encounter an accessibility barrier or need assistance using our platform, please let us know. You can report an issue by:

- **Opening a GitHub Issue:** Visit our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/stellar-marketpay/stellar-marketpay/issues/new) and open an issue with the "accessibility" label. Please include as much detail as possible, such as the page URL, the assistive technology you are using, and a description of the problem.
- **Contacting the Team:** If the issue involves sensitive information or you are unable to use GitHub, you can contact the project maintainers directly via our official communication channels (e.g., Discord or email, as listed in our repository's community guidelines).

We aim to respond to accessibility feedback within a reasonable timeframe and will do our best to address the issues raised.
